Living Adventure Map 0.2.0 (261116-02) by Sinistral

## Description

The adventure map comes alive. Wind gusts roll across the land: trees, palms, shrubs and flowers sway, broadleaf and
autumn forests lose leaves, pines drop needles and snowy pines shed snow. Every ground type has its own life: butterflies
over grass, dust devils and tumbleweed on dirt and sand, heat shimmer over deserts and lava, snowfall and sparkles on
snow, mist, bubbles and dragonflies in swamps, dripping water underground, sun glints, whitecaps and jumping fish on the
sea and in lakes, bubbling lava and rising embers. Flocks of birds cross the sky (geese in a V, songbirds, crows, gulls,
circling vultures and eagles, bats underground), soft cloud shadows drift over the map, campfires smoke. Heroes leave
hoof tracks that fade the next day and are gone after two days.

Every plant and pond is read from the graphics the game already uses, so it works with the original art, WoG Graphic
Fix, Third Upgrade Mod and JS's new objects and terrains alike.

## How to use

Unzip into `Heroes 3 ERA\Mods\` and enable the mod in the Mod Manager. All switches and strengths are in
`Mods\Living Adventure Map\Data\Living Adventure Map.ini` (each value shows its default). Change a value, then load a
save or start a game: no restart needed (except `Enabled`).

## Compatibility

- Made for ERA 3.9 with HD Mod, WoG, WoG Graphic Fix, Third Upgrade Mod (ResOunD / TUM Enchanted), JS - new objects /
  new terrains. Pure graphics: no gameplay change, savegames stay playable without the mod (the tracks are simply gone).
- Load order does not matter.
- Performance: about 1-6 ms per frame on a 1920x1080 screen. Lower `Fps` or `MaxParticles` on slow PCs.
