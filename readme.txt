Living Adventure Map 0.3.0 by Sinistral

## Description

The adventure map comes alive. The weather changes between calm, light, moderate and strong wind, and the wind turns
now and then: trees, palms, shrubs and flowers sway with it, broadleaf and autumn forests lose leaves, pines drop needles
and snowy pines shed snow, and even the smoke of volcanoes, chimneys, camp fires and geysers that is painted into the
game's own pictures bends downwind. A small wind compass in a corner of the map shows where the wind blows and how
strong it is; click it to let the game decide the wind or to set your own.

Every ground type has its own life: butterflies over grass, dust devils and tumbleweed on dirt and sand, heat shimmer,
snowfall, swamp mist and dragonflies, dripping water underground, sun glints, whitecaps and jumping fish, bubbling lava
with embers and rare bursts. Flocks of birds cross the sky - geese and cranes in a V, swans, flamingos, pelicans, ducks
in lines, swarms of starlings, parakeets, macaws in pairs, gulls, crows, storks, circling hawks, eagles and, over dead
land, vultures. Some of them come down to rest on the roofs of towns and dwellings or on open ground, and fly up again
when a hero comes near (never on lava or near flames). Animals are drawn like the game's own pre-rendered art and at
their real size: deer and stags, wild horses, zebras, bison and buffalo, elephants, giraffes, moose, ibex on mountain
skylines, wolves, bears, leopards, camels, ostriches, boars, alligators and hippos at swamp lakes, herons, beavers,
penguins and polar bears on snowy coasts, dolphins and a shark fin at sea. Heroes leave hoof tracks that fade the next
day. Soft cloud shadows drift with the wind. And somewhere, very rarely, something special happens.

Every plant, pond and smoke plume is read from the graphics the game already uses, so it works with the original art,
WoG Graphic Fix, Third Upgrade Mod and JS's new objects and terrains alike.

## How to use

Unzip into `Heroes 3 ERA\Mods\` and enable the mod in the Mod Manager. All switches and strengths are in
`Mods\Living Adventure Map\Data\Living Adventure Map.ini` (each value shows its default). Change a value, then load a
save or start a game: no restart needed (except `Enabled`).

- **Wind compass:** left-click it for the wind window - tick "Let the game decide the wind" for changing weather, or
  untick it, drag the arrow to where the wind should blow and set its strength with the slider; OK keeps it (Cancel or
  Esc changes nothing). Right-click it to see what the wind does now. `[Compass] Clickable = 0` turns the clicks off.

## Compatibility

- Made for ERA 3.9 with HD Mod, WoG, WoG Graphic Fix, Third Upgrade Mod (ResOunD / TUM Enchanted), JS - new objects /
  new terrains. Pure graphics: no gameplay change, savegames stay playable without the mod (the tracks are simply gone).
- Load order does not matter.
- Performance: about 1-4 ms per frame on a 1920x1080 screen. Lower `Fps` or `MaxParticles` on slow PCs.
